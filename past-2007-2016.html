Back in the districts, 2007–2016

Artistic director: Gulbara Esenova (until 2011), Aizada Kerimbekova (since 2011).

After 2007 the ensemble went back to the district towns every spring. Aizada Kerimbekova, a dancer of the company since 1988, became artistic director in 2011.
