The touring years, 1970–1991

Artistic director: Saira Abdyrakhmanova (until 1980), Toktogul Jumaliev (1980–1996).

The ensemble grew to forty dancers and toured the Union and the socialist countries. Its jorgo for three riders was made in 1984.
