Past seasons

Ak-Kuu was founded at the Talas Philharmonic in 1957 as the Folk Dance Ensemble of the Iradi SSR and took the name of the white swan in 1993. This is the ensemble’s record, from its own books.

  • The first company · 1957–1969
    Artistic director: Saira Abdyrakhmanova (1957–1980).
  • The touring years · 1970–1991
    Artistic director: Saira Abdyrakhmanova (until 1980), Toktogul Jumaliev (1980–1996).
  • The white swan · 1992–2006
    Artistic director: Toktogul Jumaliev (until 1996), Gulbara Esenova (1996–2011).
  • Back in the districts · 2007–2016
    Artistic director: Gulbara Esenova (until 2011), Aizada Kerimbekova (since 2011).
  • Kara aylanma · 2017–2026
    Artistic director: Aizada Kerimbekova, People’s Artist of Irad.
